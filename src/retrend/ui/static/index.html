      School enrollment

      Public-school enrollment for the district that matches a city, for every year in your school file. Enrollment is a slow, indirect signal for home prices and says nothing about school quality.

      Home prices

      Zillow home values and rents for a city, over time. Pick a measure and a time range. Zillow indexes are smoothed estimates, not sale prices.
